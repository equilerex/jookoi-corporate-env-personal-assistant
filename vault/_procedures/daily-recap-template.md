# Daily recap template

What Daily Close shows the owner before it does anything else, so they know what to talk about. Present it as a prompt, don't fill it in yourself, and wait for their answer before filing anything. This is a reminder of what's worth covering, not a rigid form: the owner talks naturally and can skip or merge fields.

```text
Recap for <the workday being closed, e.g. "today, Sep 25">:

- Focus and energy: how did the workday feel? (productive, blocked, burnt out, energized)
- What happened: the day in your own words — what you shipped, built, reviewed or debugged.
- Colleagues and meetings: 1:1s, pairing, stakeholders, decisions, anything notable from the calendar.
- Wins and blockers: merged PRs, resolved incidents, blocking dependencies, organizational friction.
- Anything on your mind: tech debt ideas, career thoughts, open loops, what comes first tomorrow.
```

Focus and energy exist because `lists/logs/work-energy.md` tracks capacity and burnout over time, so ask for them every day. Everything else is coverage, not a checklist to tick off.
